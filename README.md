## Method

The business plan follows Harvard Business School’s venture-evaluation method, still used in *The Entrepreneurial Manager* and Rock Center practice:

**William A. Sahlman, “How to Write a Great Business Plan,” *Harvard Business Review* (1997).**

A plan is a snapshot of an uncertain future. It is judged on four factors and the *fit* among them:

1. **People** — who is involved, what they know, whom they know
2. **Opportunity** — the customer, the product, the economics
3. **Context** — regulation, science, capital markets, competition, timing
4. **Risk and reward** — what can go wrong, who gets what, how uncertainty is bounded

Modern HBS practice adds testable hypotheses, cheap experiments, and a living plan—not a static 40-page novel.
