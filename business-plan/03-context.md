# 3. Context

Sahlman: *Context moves. Name the forces that can help or crush the venture.*

## Science

- TREM2 loss-of-function variants raise late-onset AD risk.
- Agonist antibodies and oral TREM2 agonists are already in or entering the clinic (e.g. antibody class; Sanofi SAR448851 Phase 2 design; Muna MNA-001).
- IV mRNA-LNPs that beat MC3 in mouse brain exist (MK16 BLNP, OS4T, ACh-functionalized particles). Most transfect neurons/astrocytes, not microglia.
- Microglia RNA delivery is strongest so far with CSF (e.g. MG-LNP siRNA) or specialized composition—not with generic vaccine LNPs.

## Regulation

- Product class: mRNA-LNP, CBER. Repeat-dose innate immunity, biodistribution, complement, PEG antibodies.
- Phase 1 in early AD: safety + CSF/imaging PD. Cognition is not the first hurdle.
- No genomic editor in this file set.

## Capital markets

- Biotech funding is selective. Dementia-specialist funds (Dementia Discovery Fund, EQT Dementia Fund) still write seed checks against a tight experiment.
- NIA SBIR/STTR reauthorized 2026; AD/ADRD Phase I cap can reach ~$700k.
- Alzheimer’s Association / ADDF are non-dilutive or hybrid paths after a data sliver exists.

## Intellectual property context

- Ionizable-lipid estate is crowded (Acuitas, Alnylam, Genevant and others).
- Freedom-to-operate on the particle is a context risk equal to biology.
- Company IP should concentrate on TREM2 mRNA design + microglia-biased use + process, after a FTO memo.

## Timing

- Anti-amyloid drugs created a commercial AD channel and a high efficacy bar.
- TREM2 is “hot enough” that a me-too agonist story will not raise. A delivery-plus-copy-number story can, if the first experiment is sharp.
