# NVL-101 scientific brief

## Asset
Human TREM2 mRNA, unmodified coding sequence (codon-optimized), formulated in a microglia-biased LNP.

## Why TREM2
CNS TREM2 is microglial. Loss-of-function alleles increase AD risk. Agonism is in the clinic. Open question: does *more receptor* on plaque-associated microglia add benefit?

## Delivery problem
Two problems, not one:
1. Reach parenchyma (BBB or bypass).
2. Transfect myeloid cells (not neurons/astrocytes).

Standard MC3/SM-102 IV LNPs fail both for this use.

## Screen design
- 20–40 compositions: MC3/SM-102 controls + 5-HT3 / ACh / LRP1-inspired ionizable lipids + PEG/charge variants.
- Readouts: IV vs intracisternal luciferase, then **sorted microglia** signal.
- One ligand (mannose or CD11b) only if composition almost works.
- Payload stage: TREM2 protein + phagocytosis in iPSC-microglia; then 5xFAD / APP-PS1.

## Kill criterion
No CNS myeloid TREM2 protein at a tolerable hepatic and cytokine dose.
