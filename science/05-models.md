# Models

## Cells

| System | Use | Do not use as |
|---|---|---|
| HEK293 / HepG2 | mRNA integrity, crude expression | Microglia decision |
| BV-2 | Cheap mouse myeloid transfection | Human TREM2 trafficking |
| Primary mouse microglia | Supporting phagocytosis | Sole go/no-go |
| **Human iPSC-microglia** | **Decision line for Aim 1** | — |
| iPSC-microglia on a BBB chip | Later translation, not seed-critical | Substitute for in vivo |
| R47H or TREM2-KD iPSC-microglia | Copy-number vs agonist wedge | Required only if commercially timely |

Source iPSC-microglia from a vendor or academic core with a published differentiation that yields P2RY12/TMEM119/TREM2-positive cells, not generic “iPSC macrophages.”

## Mice — delivery

- C57BL/6J for reporter biodistribution (both sexes, n powered for the myeloid ratio).
- Ai9 or similar only if Cre mRNA is the reporter; interpret cell type by flow, not by red skull photos.
- Human TREM2 knock-in or BAC mice become useful when the payload is human and the antibody does not see mouse TREM2. Plan the reagent before the study.

## Mice — disease (after exposure pass)

| Model | Why | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| 5xFAD | Fast plaques, used in TREM2 literature | Aggressive, not human-like tau |
| APP/PS1 | Slower, often cleaner microglia studies | Still amyloid-only |
| Trem2 R47H on an APP background | Matches the genetic rationale | Cost and breeding time |

Primary disease readouts: plaque-associated microglia density, DAM transcripts in sorted microglia, filamentous vs compact plaque, dystrophic neurites. Behavior is exploratory.

Do not lead with 3xTg cognition. Tau + amyloid + aging in one cage is too many variables for a delivery company at seed.

## Human tissue

Ex vivo human cortical slices or surgical samples are optional validation if a collaborator exists. Not a Phase I SBIR requirement.

## Translation gaps to state in every review

- Mouse BBB ≠ aged human BBB with cerebral amyloid angiopathy.
- Mouse CSF volume and turnover ≠ lumbar IT dosing in people.
- iPSC-microglia are fetal-like; plaque-associated human DAM is not fully reproduced.
- First-in-human, if ever, still needs NHP or a well-argued rodent-plus-human-cell package per CBER.
