# 5. Business model

## How value is created

Generate human-relevant evidence that TREM2 mRNA can be expressed in CNS myeloid cells at a tolerable dose, then sell either:

1. a development-stage product (NVL-101) to a strategic, or
2. a financed independent path through Phase 1/2a biomarkers.

Early value is **data**, not revenue.

## Revenue model (eventual)

- Prescription specialty biologic/nucleic-acid product for early AD.
- Administration: infusion center or CSF procedure if IV fails commercially.
- Pricing power exists only with human PD + later functional benefit.

## Cost model

- Outsourced DS (TriLink/Aldevron) + LNP CDMO (Lonza / Cytiva-PNI / Catalent / Polymun) + fill/finish.
- No internal factory at seed.
- CRO for tox and bioanalysis.

## Unit economics (hypothesis, not forecast)

Viable if: infrequent dosing, high potency in microglia, COGS in the low thousands per dose at scale, and a specialty-AD price envelope. Not modeled until dose and route are known.

## Strategic alternatives

- License particle + TREM2 mRNA to a TREM2-agonist company that lacks RNA delivery.
- Co-dev with a CNS LNP platform company that lacks a genetically locked payload.
