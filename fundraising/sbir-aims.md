## Specific Aims

Alzheimer’s disease (AD) genetics place TREM2 on microglia at the center of plaque containment. Approved anti-amyloid antibodies slow decline only modestly and do not restore microglial TREM2 function. Antibody and small-molecule TREM2 agonists act on receptor that is already present. Novellum is developing **NVL-101**, a non-integrating human TREM2 mRNA formulated in a microglia-biased lipid nanoparticle (LNP), to raise TREM2 protein on CNS myeloid cells.

**Hypothesis:** A composition-optimized LNP can deliver TREM2 mRNA to human iPSC-derived microglia and to mouse CNS myeloid cells at a dose that increases surface TREM2 and amyloid-beta phagocytosis without prohibitive hepatic or cytokine burden.

Phase I does **not** claim cognition benefit, genomic editing, or multi-organ targeting. Success is technical feasibility of payload + delivery against explicit kill criteria.

### Aim 1. Establish that TREM2 mRNA-LNPs raise functional TREM2 on human microglia

- Synthesize codon-optimized human TREM2 mRNA (cap analog, modified U as needed, dsRNA depletion).
- Formulate a small panel of research LNPs (MC3/SM-102 controls plus 8–12 CNS-motif ionizable-lipid variants).
- Transfect human iPSC-derived microglia.
- Endpoints: TREM2 mRNA (qPCR), surface TREM2 (flow), SYK phosphorylation, phagocytosis of fluorescent Aβ, viability, interferon-stimulated gene panel.

**Milestone (go):** ≥2-fold increase in surface TREM2 versus untreated and versus empty LNP, with ≥50% relative increase in Aβ phagocytosis and no catastrophic viability loss.
**Kill:** No protein or no functional phagocytosis at tolerable in-vitro dose.

### Aim 2. Quantify CNS myeloid exposure after intravenous versus CSF administration

- Encapsulate reporter mRNA (luciferase and/or Cre) and, for the lead particle, TREM2 mRNA.
- Dose wild-type mice IV and intracisternal (or equivalent CSF route), n sized for a predefined myeloid/liver ratio.
- Endpoints: whole-organ luciferase; flow or RNA on **sorted CD11b+CD45int microglia**; liver enzymes and plasma cytokines; histology for acute injury.
- In a satellite 5xFAD or APP/PS1 cohort, measure plaque-associated microglial TREM2 if Aim 1 and reporter exposure both pass.

**Milestone (go):** Statistically significant TREM2 or reporter signal in sorted microglia at a dose with acceptable ALT/AST and cytokine excursion. Record IV versus CSF ratio.
**Kill:** No myeloid signal by either route, or only neuronal/astrocyte transfection without myeloid protein.

## Expected Phase I outcome

A lead LNP composition, a preferred route (IV if the ratio allows; CSF if not), and a go/no-go package suitable for Phase II (AD-model efficacy + repeat-dose tox) and for a specialty seed raise.

## Commercialization (one paragraph for the aims page / later full section)

NVL-101 is a specialty nucleic-acid product for early, amyloid-positive AD. Phase I SBIR de-risks the delivery gate that currently blocks private capital. Follow-on: NIA Phase II, Alzheimer’s Association / ADDF translational awards, and a $3–8M seed from dementia-focused funds.
