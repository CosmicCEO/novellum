# TREM2 biology (NVL-101 context)

Numbered notes point to [references.md](references.md).

## What the receptor does

TREM2 is a transmembrane Ig-superfamily receptor on myeloid cells. In brain parenchyma it is expressed almost exclusively on microglia. Ligands include anionic lipids and damage-associated motifs; lipid sensing sustains the microglial response to Aβ.[[4]](references.md) Signaling requires the adaptor DAP12 (TYROBP) and SYK.[[7]](references.md) Downstream effects that matter for AD:

- Survival and proliferation of plaque-associated microglia
- Chemotaxis toward amyloid deposits
- Phagocytosis and plaque compaction (containment of neuritic dystrophy)[[5]](references.md)
- Metabolic fitness (lipid handling)
- Release of soluble TREM2 (sTREM2) by protease shedding of the ectodomain

sTREM2 in CSF is both a biomarker of microglial engagement and a sink that can reduce surface receptor. An mRNA product that raises *surface* TREM2 will also raise sTREM2; that is a PD marker, not automatically a liability, but it must be measured.

A plaque-associated “DAM” transcriptional program is TREM2-dependent in its second step.[[6]](references.md)

## Genetics that lock the target

- Rare coding variants (including R47H) increase late-onset AD risk.[[1]](references.md)[[2]](references.md)
- Complete TREM2/DAP12 loss causes Nasu–Hakola disease (PLOSL) with osteoclast and CNS myeloid failure — proof the pathway is not optional.[[7]](references.md)
- The genetic direction of effect is **loss of function → more disease**. Gain-of-signaling is therefore the therapeutic direction.

## Why mRNA is a different lever

| Modality | What it does |
|---|---|
| Agonist mAb / small molecule | Occupies and clusters *existing* surface TREM2 |
| NVL-101 mRNA | Increases TREM2 *copy number* on transfected microglia |

**Working hypothesis:** early, plaque-bearing cortex contains a microglia subset that is TREM2-low relative to demand. Raising WT protein on that subset improves DAM/plaque-associated programs.[[5]](references.md)[[6]](references.md)

**Falsifiers:** (1) transfected microglia do not increase phagocytosis; (2) extra TREM2 increases inflammatory damage; (3) only homeostatic microglia take up LNPs, missing the plaque niche.

## Stage dependence (do not ignore)

TREM2 effects on amyloid pathology are stage-dependent in mice: deficiency can look helpful early and harmful later.[[3]](references.md)[[12]](references.md) NVL-101 is therefore an **early-AD / amyloid-first** asset. Patient selection in any future trial: amyloid PET or CSF Aβ-positive, early clinical stage.

## Competitive pharmacology (context for assays)

- Antibody agonists (e.g. AL002 class) and oral agonists act on receptor that is already present.
- NVL-101 must show incremental biology versus agonism alone. The dish experiment that matters is WT mRNA in TREM2-low or variant microglia.

## Off-target biology to watch

- Peripheral myeloid TREM2 (osteoclasts, macrophages) if IV LNPs transfect them.[[7]](references.md)
- Over-phagocytosis of synapses if TREM2 is driven too high in the wrong state.
- Interferon programs from the mRNA/LNP itself. Empty-LNP and non-TREM2 mRNA controls are mandatory.
