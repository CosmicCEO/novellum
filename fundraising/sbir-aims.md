## Specific Aims

TREM2 loss-of-function variants raise AD risk. The agonist antibody AL002 engaged CSF TREM2 biology in early AD, missed CDR-SB, and produced ARIA-like MRI. NVL-101 tests a different lever: **more WT TREM2 protein** on myeloid cells that are receptor-low, delivered by an ionizable LNP. Myeloid tropism is a screen goal. IV hepatic bias is expected; **CSF is the Phase I exposure route.**

Phase I does not claim cognition, editing, or an IV product.

### Aim 1. Show copy-number beats occupancy in TREM2-low human microglia

- Synthesize codon-optimized human TREM2 mRNA (Cap 1, modified U as needed, dsRNA-depleted) and formulate 2–3 published MG-class LNPs plus MC3/SM-102 controls.
- Transfect WT and R47H or TREM2-low human iPSC-microglia.
- Endpoints: surface TREM2, sTREM2, ligand-driven pSYK, Aβ phagocytosis, ePtdSer/synapse-eating control, viability, ISG panel (IFIT1, MX1, RSAD2). Include a TREM2 agonist comparator and empty LNP.

**Go:** ≥2× surface TREM2 vs empty LNP and vs irrelevant mRNA, functional phagocytosis, **and** a win versus agonist on the R47H / TREM2-low line at a non-toxic, non-ISG-storm dose.
**Kill:** No protein, or agonist wins the variant line.

### Aim 2. Quantify sorted CNS myeloid protein after CSF (IV as comparator)

- Encapsulate Cre or mouse Trem2 (delivery RNA) and, on the lead particle, human TREM2 mRNA.
- Dose mice intracisternal (or equivalent CSF route) and IV.
- Endpoints: protein in **FACS-sorted parenchymal myeloid cells**; liver enzymes; cytokines; dose-2 anti-PEG if a second dose fits the calendar; PSD-95 / drebrin on a satellite amyloid cohort only if exposure passes.

**Go:** Significant myeloid protein after CSF at acceptable ALT/AST and cytokines. Record the IV ratio; do not require an IV win.
**Kill:** No myeloid protein by CSF, or only neuron/astrocyte transfection.

## Expected Phase I outcome

A lead composition, a CSF exposure package, a gated dish result versus agonism, and a go/no-go for Phase II (humanized-TREM2 × amyloid at two ages + repeat-dose safety in [science/10-safety.md](../science/10-safety.md)).

## Commercialization (one paragraph)

NVL-101 is a specialty nucleic-acid candidate. Phase I de-risks the two gates that block private capital: CSF myeloid protein and a copy-number wedge after AL002. Follow-on: NIA Phase II, ADDF, $3–8M seed.
