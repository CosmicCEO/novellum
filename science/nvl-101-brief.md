# NVL-101 scientific brief

Expanded package: [README.md](README.md). Citations: [references.md](references.md).

## Asset
Human TREM2 mRNA, codon-optimized WT isoform 1, formulated in a microglia-biased LNP.

## Why TREM2
CNS TREM2 is microglial. Heterozygous loss-of-function alleles increase AD risk.[[1]](references.md)[[2]](references.md) Lipid sensing and plaque compaction are TREM2-dependent.[[4]](references.md)[[5]](references.md) Agonism is in the clinic. Open question: does *more receptor* on plaque-associated microglia add benefit?

## Delivery problem
Two problems, not one:
1. Reach parenchyma (BBB or bypass).
2. Transfect myeloid cells (not neurons/astrocytes).

Standard MC3 IV LNPs are a liver-biased clinical baseline, not a microglia product.[[10]](references.md)

## Screen design
- 20–40 compositions: MC3/SM-102 controls + CNS-motif ionizable lipids.
- Readouts: IV vs intracisternal reporter, then **sorted microglia** signal.
- Payload stage: TREM2 protein + phagocytosis in iPSC-microglia; then 5xFAD / APP-PS1.[[11]](references.md)[[12]](references.md)

## Kill criterion
No CNS myeloid TREM2 protein at a tolerable hepatic and cytokine dose.
