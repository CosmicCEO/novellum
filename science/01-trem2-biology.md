# TREM2 biology (NVL-101 context)

## What the receptor does

TREM2 is a transmembrane Ig-superfamily receptor on myeloid cells. In brain parenchyma it is expressed almost exclusively on microglia. Ligands include anionic lipids, lipoprotein particles, Aβ-associated debris, and other damage-associated motifs. Signaling requires the adaptor DAP12 (TYROBP) and SYK, then PI3K–AKT and calcium flux. Downstream effects that matter for AD:

- Survival and proliferation of plaque-associated microglia
- Chemotaxis toward amyloid deposits
- Phagocytosis and plaque compaction (containment of neuritic dystrophy)
- Metabolic fitness (lipid handling, oxidative phosphorylation)
- Release of soluble TREM2 (sTREM2) by protease shedding of the ectodomain

sTREM2 in CSF is both a biomarker of microglial engagement and a sink that can reduce surface receptor. An mRNA product that raises *surface* TREM2 will also raise sTREM2; that is a PD marker, not automatically a liability, but it must be measured.

## Genetics that lock the target

- Rare coding variants (R47H, R62H, H157Y, T66M and others) increase late-onset AD risk. Most are partial loss of ligand binding, surface expression, or shedding.
- Complete TREM2 loss causes Nasu–Hakola disease (PLOSL), a leukoencephalopathy — proof the pathway is not optional in human myeloid CNS biology.
- The genetic direction of effect is **loss of function → more disease**. Gain-of-signaling is therefore the therapeutic direction. That is why agonist antibodies and oral agonists exist.

## Why mRNA is a different lever

| Modality | What it does |
|---|---|
| Agonist mAb / small molecule | Occupies and clusters *existing* surface TREM2 |
| NVL-101 mRNA | Increases TREM2 *copy number* on transfected microglia |

These are not substitutes. Cells that have downregulated TREM2, or that express a hypomorphic variant, may respond poorly to an agonist and still respond to new wild-type protein. Cells that already have abundant receptor may need agonism more than extra copies.

**Working hypothesis:** early, plaque-bearing cortex contains a microglia subset that is TREM2-low relative to demand. Raising WT protein on that subset improves DAM/plaque-associated programs.

**Falsifiers:** (1) transfected microglia do not increase phagocytosis; (2) extra TREM2 increases inflammatory damage; (3) only homeostatic microglia take up LNPs, missing the plaque niche.

## Stage dependence (do not ignore)

TREM2 help in amyloid models is strongest while plaques are being seeded and compacted. In some tau-forward models TREM2 reduction can look protective. NVL-101 is therefore an **early-AD / amyloid-first** asset, not a late-tau or mixed-dementia catch-all. Patient selection in any future trial: amyloid PET or CSF Aβ-positive, early clinical stage.

## Competitive pharmacology (context for assays)

- Antibody agonists (e.g. AL002 class): clinical target engagement via CSF sTREM2 / CSF1R axis; efficacy still the open question.
- Oral agonists (e.g. Sanofi SAR448851, Muna MNA-001): brain-penetrant small molecules; Phase 2 designs exist.
- NVL-101 must show *incremental* biology versus “just agonize.” The dish experiment that matters is WT mRNA in TREM2-low or variant microglia, not only in TREM2-high BV-2 cells.

## Off-target biology to watch

- Peripheral myeloid TREM2 (osteoclasts, macrophages, adipose-associated myeloid cells) if IV LNPs transfect them.
- Over-phagocytosis of synapses if TREM2 is driven too high in the wrong state.
- Interferon programs from the mRNA/LNP itself mimicking “TREM2 activation.” Empty-LNP and non-TREM2 mRNA controls are mandatory.
