# NVL-101 scientific brief

Expanded package: [README.md](README.md). Citations: [references.md](references.md).

## Asset
Human TREM2 mRNA, codon-optimized WT isoform 1, in an ionizable LNP. Myeloid tropism is a **screen goal**, not a current property.

## Why TREM2
CNS TREM2 is microglial. Heterozygous loss-of-function alleles increase AD risk.[[1]](references.md)[[2]](references.md) Lipid sensing and plaque compaction are TREM2-dependent.[[4]](references.md)[[5]](references.md) The TREM2 agonist mAb AL002 engaged the target in CSF and missed CDR-SB, with ARIA-like MRI.[[16]](references.md) Open question: does *more receptor* in TREM2-low / R47H cells add function occupancy did not?

## Delivery problem
Two problems, not one. Standard MC3 IV LNPs are liver-biased.[[10]](references.md)

**Operating rule:** CSF is the PD-enabling route. IV runs in parallel and cannot kill the biology. Reproduce 2–3 published MG-class compositions; do not raffle 40 lipids.

## Screen design
- Delivery cargo: Cre or mouse Trem2. Product cargo: human WT TREM2, same particle family.
- Readout: **sorted parenchymal myeloid protein** after CSF, then IV ratio.
- Dish: WT and **gated** R47H / TREM2-low iPSC-microglia vs agonist + empty LNP.
- Disease: humanized TREM2 × amyloid at two ages after exposure.[[11]](references.md)[[12]](references.md)
- Safety on the same animals: synapses, dose-2 complement, periphery, vessels, ISG. [10-safety.md](10-safety.md)

## Kill criterion
No CNS myeloid TREM2 protein at a tolerable dose by CSF *and* IV, **or** agonist wins the R47H dish, **or** synaptic markers drop at the PD dose.
