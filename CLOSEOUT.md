# CLOSED — NVL-101 infeasible as a product

**Status:** Closed. Do not raise, partner, or run a seed screen on this asset.
**Date:** 2026-09-28
**Decision owner:** founder close after adversarial review + AL002 readout.

## Verdict

NVL-101 (human TREM2 mRNA in an ionizable LNP for early AD) is **not a viable company thesis**. Genetics for TREM2 remain real. The product does not clear a 50:50 bar for technical success or for regulatory success. Stacked unsolved problems plus a failed occupancy class make a raise dishonest.

This repo is an archive of the argument, not a living development plan.

## Why closed (plain)

1. **Delivery is unsolved.** IV LNPs go to liver. CSF is a different drug and still unproven for *plaque-associated* microglia in an aged amyloid brain.
2. **The human TREM2 experiment already ran.** AL002 occupied the receptor, moved CSF markers, missed CDR-SB, did not clear amyloid PET, and caused ARIA-like MRI. Occupancy is not an open door.
3. **Copy-number is unproven and may be empty.** Plaque microglia are often already TREM2-high. Extra WT receptor has not been shown to beat an agonist in TREM2-weak cells.
4. **Safety can veto a PD win.** Synapses, dose-2 complement, peripheral myeloid, ARIA-like vessels, interferon — any one kills a “clean” protein signal.
5. **Regulatory path is worse than the mouse path.** CBER mRNA-LNP into AD, after AL002 ARIA, with a small honest genetic slice, is not a coin flip.

## What is not claimed

- TREM2 biology is false. It is not.
- No one should study microglia RNA delivery. That is a lab question, not this company.
- A future asset with data could reopen a *different* TREM2 program. This one is closed until such data exist *outside* a Novellum raise.

## What stops

- Seed or Series A narrative for NVL-101
- SBIR submission of the current aims as a product plan (archive only)
- Adding BDNF, Cas9, stem-cell ligands, Evonik-scale CMC, or dual payloads
- Slides that say microglia-biased, preferred IV, q4–12 week, or sTREM2-as-PD

## What remains useful

The science files are a map of *why* it died: red team, AL002 numbers, safety holds, banned language. Keep them. Do not staff them.

## Reopen rule (explicit and high)

Reopen only if an independent lab shows **sorted parenchymal myeloid TREM2 protein** after a tolerable CSF or IV dose **and** WT mRNA beats agonist + empty LNP on R47H / TREM2-low human microglia **and** synaptic markers hold. Until all three exist, the file stays closed.
