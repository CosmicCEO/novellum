# NVL-101 scientific brief — CLOSED

**Status: infeasible as a product. Archive only.** Close-out: [../CLOSEOUT.md](../CLOSEOUT.md).

Expanded package: [README.md](README.md). Citations: [references.md](references.md).

## Asset (historical)
Human TREM2 mRNA, codon-optimized WT isoform 1, in an ionizable LNP. Myeloid tropism was a screen goal, never a demonstrated property.

## Why it was attempted
CNS TREM2 is microglial. Loss-of-function alleles raise AD risk.[[1]](references.md)[[2]](references.md) AL002 occupied TREM2, moved CSF markers, missed CDR-SB, caused ARIA-like MRI.[[16]](references.md) The open question was whether *more receptor* in TREM2-low cells would add what occupancy did not.

## Why it is closed
Delivery unsolved. Occupancy class already failed in humans. Copy-number unproven. Safety vetoes (synapses, complement, vessels, interferon) sit on the same critical path. Joint P(technical) and P(regulatory) both below even. See [08-adversarial-review.md](08-adversarial-review.md), [10-safety.md](10-safety.md), [11-al002-clinical.md](11-al002-clinical.md).

## Do not restart from this brief
No raise, no SBIR-as-product, no payload add-ons. Reopen rule is in CLOSEOUT.md and requires data this company does not have.
