# 4. Risk and reward

Sahlman: *What can go wrong? Who bears it? What is the upside if it goes right?*

## Deal-killing risks

| Risk | How it kills | Bound |
|---|---|---|
| No sorted myeloid protein after CSF | Biology never starts | Reproduce MG-class LNPs; stop if CSF misses |
| IV hepatic only | Commercial IV TPP dies | Expected; CSF can still be the product |
| Agonist beats WT mRNA on R47H dish | Copy-number wedge empty | Gated experiment; AL002 already failed clinically[[16]](../science/references.md) |
| Synaptic marker drop at PD dose | Activation is the wrong kind | Ranked kill ([10-safety.md](../science/10-safety.md)) |
| ARIA-like / CAA signal | TREM2-class human risk | Vessel screen; APOE4 plan; no anti-amyloid combo in FIH |
| Dose-2 ABC / complement leak | Redose impossible | PEG-lipid choice; measure anti-PEG |
| Chronic ISG at PD dose | Particle impersonates target | Chemistry matrix; empty-LNP subtract |
| Peripheral bone/liver injury on CSF dose | No safe route | Histology on first repeat-dose |
| Lipid FTO | Cannot manufacture | Counsel letter before IND spend |
| Cash before the gate | Company expires mid-screen | Seed sized to CSF protein + R47H dish |

## Reward if gates hit

- Seed holders own a CSF-validated TREM2 mRNA particle plus a dish result agonist antibodies did not substitute.
- Series A on PD and a written safety package, not on slides.
- Partner path is a large-cap AD franchise buying a *different modality after AL002*, not another agonist.

## Risk allocation

- Founders: time and early dilution.
- Seed: experiment risk (CSF protein + R47H vs agonist + safety panel).
- Grants: non-dilutive share of the same experiment.
- Series A: NHP IT/ICM if CSF is the product, CMC, FIH.
